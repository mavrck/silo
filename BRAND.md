# Silo RSS

**Tagline:** Grain by grain.

## Concept

Silo RSS treats feed aggregation as a harvest: individual RSS items are grain, collected and stored for later. The mark is a low-poly grain silo — dome, ridged body, hopper, spout, and legs — instead of the generic RSS broadcast icon. Wordmark: **Silo** (dark brown) + **RSS** (forest green), set in bold sans-serif.

Logo file: `logo.svg` (project root).

## Usage

- Minimum clear space around the badge: roughly half the badge's width on all sides.
- Don't recolor the facets individually — treat gold/amber/brown as a fixed set.
- On dark or busy backgrounds, use the badge on its forest-green field rather than dropping the badge fill out.
- Favicon and app-icon crops should use the badge only (no wordmark) — see `favicon` work in progress.
